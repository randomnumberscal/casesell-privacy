Casesell Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Casesell: Upgrade Your Skins can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Casesell account, send an email to:

YOUR_EMAIL_HERE

Please use the email address connected to your Casesell account whenever possible.

Use the following subject:

Casesell Account Deletion Request
Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted

When your account deletion request is verified and processed, data associated with your Casesell account that is stored by us may be deleted, including:

  • Your Casesell account
  • Email address associated with your account
  • User profile information
  • Nickname and profile information
  • Synchronized inventory data
  • Synchronized game progress
  • Account-related game history stored on our backend

Data stored on your device

Some game information may be stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

Some information related to purchases, subscriptions, authentication, advertising, or transactions may also be processed by third-party services such as Google Play, Google, RevenueCat, AdMob, or Supabase.

Information that those providers are required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction-record purposes may remain subject to their own retention policies.

Processing your request

We may ask you to verify that you own the account before deleting it. This helps prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Account deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after verification.
